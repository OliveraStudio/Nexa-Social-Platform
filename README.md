# Nexa — Connect, Share, Discover.

**Nexa** is a modern social networking application inspired by X (formerly Twitter), designed to bring people together through conversations, ideas, stories, and real-time interactions.

Nexa provides a clean, intuitive, and modern social media experience where users can express themselves, share their thoughts, discover interesting content, and connect with communities.

Built with simplicity, performance, and user experience in mind, Nexa aims to create a comfortable digital space for meaningful conversations and everyday social interactions.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏠 Home Feed
- Personalized timeline for discovering posts.
- View posts from followed accounts.
- Explore the latest conversations and updates.
- Smooth feed navigation.
- Pull-to-refresh functionality.

### 📝 Posts & Microblogging
- Create and publish text-based posts.
- Share images and media attachments.
- Reply to existing posts.
- Repost content from other users.
- Like and interact with posts.
- View post engagement and interaction counts.

- Like and unlike posts.

- Search for users and posts.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Nexa focuses on a modern, minimalistic, and user-friendly interface.

- **Minimalism:** Clean layouts with carefully organized content.
- **Modern UI:** Contemporary components and intuitive navigation.
- **Consistency:** A unified design language across screens.
- **Accessibility:** Readable typography and clear visual hierarchy.
- **Performance:** Smooth transitions and responsive interactions.
- **User Experience:** Simple navigation that keeps conversations at the center.

Nexa aims to provide a familiar social networking experience while maintaining its own visual identity.
